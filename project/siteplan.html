TradeSmart Guide - Site Plan

Site Name

TradeSmart Guide

Why this name? The name "TradeSmart Guide" conveys intelligent, strategic trading guidance that helps users make informed investment decisions. It's approachable yet professional, suggesting both expertise and accessibility for family and friends who want reliable trading strategies without constant market monitoring. The name emphasizes smart planning and guidance rather than risky speculation.

Domain availability: tradesmart-guide.com (example domain)

Site Purpose

TradeSmart Guide is a bare-bones web application designed to help busy individuals create personalized stock trading plans without requiring constant market attention. The site prompts users for their financial goals, risk tolerance, and investment timeline, then generates a customized portfolio plan based on a proven trading algorithm. Users receive estimated performance projections over time and can view their recommended asset allocation.

The unique feature is the integrated investing platform that opens directly within the page, allowing users to implement their personalized trading plan immediately. This webapp makes sophisticated trading strategies accessible to family and friends who trust the algorithm but can't dedicate time to active market monitoring during their workday. The site provides peace of mind through automated planning and streamlined execution.

Scenarios

These questions represent the target audience's needs and will drive the site's content:

  1. How do I create a trading plan that fits my financial goals and risk tolerance?

    A busy professional wants to invest but doesn't have time to actively trade. They need guidance on building a portfolio based on their savings goals, timeline (retirement, house down payment, etc.), and comfort level with market volatility.

  2. What kind of returns can I expect from my trading plan and how has the algorithm performed historically?

    Users want to see projected portfolio growth over time and understand the track record of the trading algorithm. They're looking for estimated results, historical performance data, and realistic expectations for their investment strategy.

  3. How do I implement my trading plan and actually buy the recommended stocks?

    After receiving their personalized plan, users need a seamless way to execute trades. They want to access their investing platform directly from the site to purchase recommended assets without switching apps or losing their plan details.
